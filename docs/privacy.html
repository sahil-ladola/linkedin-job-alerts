Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

LinkedIn Job Alerts is a personal tool with a single user, its developer. This page describes what it does with data from the Google account it is connected to.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: it reads emails sent by jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com in the connected Gmail account. It does not send, delete or change emails.
  • Google Sheets: it adds and updates rows in one Google Sheet owned by the same account.

How the data is used

From each alert email the workflow extracts the job id, title, company, location and link of every job listed, and writes them to the Google Sheet. Nothing else from the mailbox is used.

Where the data is stored

The workflow runs in a self-hosted n8n instance on the developer's own computer. The extracted job rows are stored in the developer's own Google Sheet. The sign-in tokens are stored in n8n's encrypted credential store on that computer.

Who the data is shared with

The title, company and location of each job are sent to the Google Gemini API so that the job can be sorted into a category. No other data is sent to any third party. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Rows stay in the Google Sheet until the developer deletes them. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the workflow from reading Gmail or writing to Sheets.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub page.
